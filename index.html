How it works

Simple 4-step process designed for safety, speed, and environmental integrity.

1

We Collect

Schedule a pickup online or by phone. Our team collects e-waste from your doorstep or facility.

2

We Disassemble

Devices are sorted, data is destroyed, and units are carefully disassembled at our facility.

3

We Recycle

Materials are channeled to authorized large-scale recycling partners for safe recovery.

4

You Get Certified

Receive a certificate of recycling or destruction for your records and compliance needs.

The Vision Behind CircuitCycle

A young entrepreneur's commitment to meaningful societal change.

CircuitCycle was envisioned and founded by Parth Saxena, a young entrepreneur determined to use innovation as a vehicle for social good. Recognizing the devastating ecological and health toll of unscientific electronic waste disposal, Parth built CircuitCycle with a singular core belief: business exists to serve society and safeguard our shared home.

“When youth step forward with a genuine willingness to give back, sustainable progress is born. Every device we recover responsibly is a step toward healthier communities and a greener future.”

— Parth Saxena, Founder
