How the program works

A complete closed loop: we grow the forest, verify the carbon, and trade the credits — you get the offsets and the reports.

Managed Forest Programs

We acquire, plant, and maintain forest parcels on behalf of your enterprise. Native species, geo-tagged plantations, and survival-rate guarantees.

  • Site selection & soil assessment
  • Native species plantation
  • 5–15 year maintenance contracts
  • Drone & satellite monitoring reports

Carbon Credit Generation

Your managed forests are measured, verified, and registered to generate certified carbon credits that offset your corporate emissions.

  • Sequestration measurement & verification
  • Registry-recognized credits
  • Annual carbon audit reports
  • ESG & sustainability report support

Credit Trading Desk

Buy credits to offset your footprint, or sell surplus credits your forests generate. We broker the exchange end to end.

  • Buy-side offset procurement
  • Sell-side credit monetization
  • Transparent market pricing
  • Retirement certificates for offsets

Compliance & Reporting

Complete documentation for regulators, auditors, and stakeholders — from plantation records to credit retirement proofs.

  • Third-party verification coordination
  • Board-ready ESG disclosures
  • Full chain-of-custody records
  • Dedicated compliance manager
