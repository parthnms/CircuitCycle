About CircuitCycle

We believe every circuit board, battery, and screen deserves a second life — not a landfill.

Our story

CircuitCycle was founded on a simple observation: households and businesses alike had drawers, storerooms, and warehouses full of old electronics — and no easy, trustworthy way to get rid of them. Most of it ended up in informal channels, burned or dumped, poisoning soil and groundwater.

We built a different path. Today we collect e-waste directly from homes, offices, and factories; disassemble it at our own facility with trained technicians; destroy data with certified processes; and channel every recovered material to large, authorized recycling partners. Devices that still have life in them are refurbished and resold — everything else is responsibly recycled.

The result: our customers get a hassle-free, documented, and often profitable way to dispose of electronics — and the planet gets a little cleaner with every pickup.

Leadership With Purpose

Building a cleaner tomorrow through compassionate entrepreneurship.

At the helm of CircuitCycle is Parth Saxena, a young, dynamic entrepreneur whose venture was born out of a relentless desire to create lasting, positive change for society. Observing how electronic waste was quietly poisoning groundwater and burdening local communities, Parth resolved to step up with a solution that prioritized human well-being and ecological balance over short-term gain.

Believing that the true measure of enterprise lies in its societal contribution, Parth has steered CircuitCycle into an authorized force for zero-landfill disposal and reforestation. His visionary approach integrates grassroots upliftment with cutting-edge circular economy practices — empowering local workforces, educating citizens on toxic hazards, and proving that youth-led leadership can tackle the world’s toughest environmental challenges.

“True entrepreneurship is defined by service to society. We are not merely managing discarded silicon and copper — we are protecting the air our communities breathe, the soil that sustains us, and the planet we pass on. When innovation is guided by a genuine intent to do good, sustainable transformation becomes inevitable.”

— Parth Saxena, Founder & Chief Visionary
