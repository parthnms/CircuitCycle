Our Services

End-to-end e-waste solutions — from the moment you hand over a device to its final responsible recycling.

Household E-Waste Pickup

Convenient doorstep collection of your old electronics. We accept phones, laptops, TVs, refrigerators, washing machines, and small appliances.

  • Doorstep pickup at your convenience
  • All household electronics accepted
  • Fair value paid for reusable devices
  • Disposal service for a small fee

Corporate IT Asset Disposal (ITAD)

End-of-life management for office IT equipment. We handle everything from a single office refresh to enterprise-wide asset retirement.

  • Bulk pickup of computers, servers & peripherals
  • Certified data destruction with reports
  • Asset valuation and buyback options
  • Compliance documentation for audits

Industrial E-Waste Management

Large-scale handling of industrial electronics, control panels, machinery circuit boards, and production scrap.

  • On-site assessment and collection
  • Scheduled recurring pickups
  • Hazardous component handling
  • Full regulatory compliance support

Certified Data Destruction

Secure wiping, degaussing, and physical destruction of storage media — with serial-number-level certificates.

  • Software wiping to industry standards (NIST 800-88)
  • Physical shredding of magnetic drives & SSDs
  • Certificate per device serial number
  • On-site destruction available

Disassembly & Material Recovery

Devices are carefully disassembled at our facility. Metals, plastics, and components are sorted and channeled to authorized large-scale recycling partners.

  • Manual disassembly by trained technicians
  • Precious metal and copper recovery
  • Partnerships with certified recyclers
  • Zero-landfill guarantee

Compliance & Documentation

Stay on the right side of e-waste regulations with complete paperwork for every transaction.

  • Collection receipts and manifests
  • Recycling certificates
  • EPR compliance support
  • Annual waste reports for businesses
